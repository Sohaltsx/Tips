Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what Sandeep Singh ("we") collects when you send a tip to Sohal Gaming.

What we collect

  • Name you type (shown on stream), amount, and message.
  • Order and payment IDs and payment status from our payment gateway (Cashfree).
  • A one-way hash of your IP address, kept for up to 1 hour, to prevent abuse (rate limiting).

The mobile number you enter is passed straight to Cashfree (it is required for the payment) and is not stored in our database or shown on stream. Your card, UPI and bank details are collected by Cashfree, not by us, under Cashfree's privacy policy.

How we use it

  • To show your tip alert on the live stream (via Streamlabs).
  • To keep records of payments and handle disputes or legal requirements.

Sharing

We do not sell your data. It is shared only with the services needed to run tips: Cashfree (payments), Supabase (database hosting) and Streamlabs (on-stream alerts). Your name and message become public when shown on stream.

Retention

Tip records are kept as long as needed for accounting and legal purposes.

Your choices

You can use a nickname instead of your real name. To ask us to delete or correct your tip details, contact us using the details on the Contact page.

Cookies

This site does not use tracking or advertising cookies. Cashfree Checkout may use cookies needed to process payments.
