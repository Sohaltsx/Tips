Refund & Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Tips to Sohal Gaming are voluntary gifts to support the stream. Tips are generally non-refundable and cannot be cancelled once the payment succeeds.

When we will refund

  • Duplicate payment: you were charged more than once for the same tip.
  • Wrong amount by mistake: for example ₹5000 instead of ₹500, if reported within 48 hours.
  • Money debited but payment failed: these are normally reversed by your bank or Cashfree automatically within 5–7 working days.

How to request

Email us within 7 days of the payment with the payment reference shown after paying (or your UPI / bank reference), amount, date and the name you used. See the Contact page.

Processing time

Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5–7 working days, depending on your bank.

No refunds for

  • A message not shown or read aloud on stream (for example because it was filtered, or stream issues).
  • Changing your mind after the tip.
